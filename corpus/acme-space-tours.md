# Acme Space Tours — Passenger Guide

Welcome aboard! Acme Space Tours has been flying paying passengers beyond the
Kármán line since 2029. This guide covers our destinations, fleet, safety
program, and booking policies. Last updated March 2026.

## Destinations

### Lunar Highlands Getaway
Our most popular trip: a 3-day loop around the Moon with a low-orbit sunrise
over the Tycho crater. Passengers enjoy 6 hours of guided low-gravity hiking
in the Lunar Highlands, an Earthrise viewing deck with floor-to-ceiling
windows, and a celebratory dinner on the return leg. Departs monthly from
Boca Chica. Price: **$12,500 per person**, all-inclusive.

### Olympus Mons Expedition (Mars)
The flagship 14-day expedition. After a 6-day cruise, you spend 2 full days
on the Martian surface near Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the solar
system, with pressurized rover excursions and a guided hike to the caldera
rim viewpoint. Surface suits and training are included. Mars launch windows
open every 26 months; the next departure is November 2027. Price: **$48,000
per person**, including the 2-day pre-flight training camp in Houston.

### Europa Ice Caves
A 9-day voyage to Jupiter's moon Europa. Highlights include a tethered
submersible dive beneath the ice shell, where cameras capture bioluminescent
plumes, and a flyby of Io's volcanic plains on the return journey. Because
of Jupiter's radiation belts, this trip carries a lifetime radiation dose
roughly equal to 3 years of natural background exposure, which is disclosed
and tracked per passenger. Departs twice a year. Price: **$36,000 per
person**.

## Fleet

### Aurora
Our lunar workhorse. Carries 12 passengers plus 2 crew, with panoramic
observation blisters and a centrifuge lounge that simulates lunar gravity
for acclimatization. Aurora has completed 84 incident-free lunar loops.

### Kepler's Dream
The deep-space cruiser for Mars and Europa voyages. Carries 8 passengers in
private cabins, plus a galley run by a real chef, a gym with resistance
equipment, and a science lab where passengers can assist with experiments.
Cruise speed: 28 km/s with continuous low-thrust ion drive.

### Selene
A 4-passenger private charter yacht for custom itineraries — proposals,
anniversaries, or just bragging rights. Selene charters start at **$95,000
per day** with a 3-day minimum, and include a dedicated concierge.

## Safety & Training

Every passenger completes a 2-day pre-flight training camp in Houston
covering emergency procedures, suit operation, and microgravity adaptation.
A flight-surgeon medical clearance is required within 60 days of departure;
passengers must be at least 18 years old.

Our safety record: 212 crewed flights, zero loss-of-crew events, and a
98.7% on-time departure rate. Each vehicle carries redundant life support,
two independent abort systems, and is inspected for 400 hours between
flights. In-flight radiation exposure is monitored with personal dosimeters
and published in your post-flight report.

## Pricing & Booking FAQ

**How do I book?** Reserve online with a 20% deposit. The balance is due
60 days before departure. Full refunds are available up to 90 days before
launch; 50% refunds up to 30 days.

**What's included?** All meals (chef-prepared, including vegetarian and
kosher options), training, flight suit rental, insurance, and ground
transfers. Not included: pre-flight hotel nights and souvenirs from the
orbital gift shop.

**Baggage allowance?** 25 kg per passenger in soft-sided bags. Hard-shell
suitcases are not permitted in the cabin.

**Is there wifi?** Yes — high-bandwidth laser link, with about a 1.3-second
delay on lunar trips and up to 45 minutes of delay near Mars. Streaming
is throttled during critical maneuvers.

**Can I bring my pet?** Unfortunately not. Animals are not permitted on
any Acme Space Tours flight, with the sole exception of certified service
animals on lunar loops.

**Do I need to be fit?** You need basic mobility and the ability to climb
a ladder in a 12 kg suit. The centrifuge test spins you to 3g for 30
seconds — most healthy adults pass comfortably.

**What happens if my flight is scrubbed?** Weather or technical scrubs are
rebooked on the next available departure at no charge, plus a $500 onboard
credit for the inconvenience.
